SCIFI - POWER/READOUT CHAIN AND MONITORING SURFACE
====================================================================

Planning-only doc, created 2026-07-30. Companion to custom_page.txt,
NOT a replacement: custom_page.txt still owns the page architecture
(two-page split, mega-sections, the FEB/LV/HV tables, the granularity
model). This doc owns two things custom_page.txt does not cover:

  (1) the PRECONDITION CHAIN - what has to be true, in what order,
      before SciFi can be powered and before data can flow, and
  (2) the MONITORING SURFACE - the full set of ODB quantities that
      describe the SciFi detector, most of which no doc mentioned
      before today.

Everything here is grounded in docs/odb_300726_112101.json (dump taken
2026-07-30 11:21) unless stated otherwise. Where a claim is inference
rather than readback, it says so.

Origin: a working session on 2026-07-30. The user had discovered that
LV/HV/FEB are not independently controllable - there is a dependency
ladder above them - and asked for the whole monitorable/operable
surface to be established before any of it goes into the page.


0) WHY THIS EXISTS - the one-line version
--------------------------------------------------------------------
A shifter looking at today's page design would see "LV off" and have no
way to learn that the LV is off because a DCDC crate is unpowered,
because the crate's own supply channel is off, three levels further up.
And they could have every power row green and still take no data,
because readout_scifi is False on three readout boards.

The page's job, per the user's framing: "capire al volo dov'e il collo
di bottiglia e sistemarlo."


1) THE CHAIN HAS TWO BRANCHES
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Established 2026-07-30. This supersedes the implicit assumption in
custom_page.txt section 3.1 that FEB/LV/HV are three peer categories -
they are peers, but they all sit at the BOTTOM of a ladder, and there
is a second, independent ladder for readout. The two converge on "data
is flowing".

POWER BRANCH
  0. TDK generator          TDKStaging/Settings/Output On[0,1]
                            ("SciFi & Tile US" / "DS")
                            STATE 2026-07-30: ON, CV mode, 20 V, 0.00 A

  1. Interlock permissive   TDKStaging/Settings/Interlock Trigger[]
                            + Fault Register[]
                            STATE: OK on the SciFi channels

  2. DCDC crate mains       PowerDistStaging, device "Slow Control":
                            ch 96-99  US DCDC C1..C4
                            ch 112-115 DS DCDC C5..C8
                            STATE: Output On = 0 on all 8

  3. SMB/LDO distribution   PowerDistStaging, device "SciFi & Tile":
                            ch 0-5   SMB_US_0-1 .. SMB_US_10-11
                            ch 16-21 SMB_DS_0-1 .. SMB_DS_10-11
                            ch 15 US_LDO, ch 31 DS_LDO
                            STATE: Output On = 0 on all

  4. DCDC crate power       DCDCCrates/Settings/Enabled[0-7]
                            then    /Settings/Crate On[0-7]
                            STATE: Enabled all True (changed from all
                            False between the 24/07 and 30/07 dumps),
                            Crate On all False

  5. Per-Feb LV             DCDCCrates channel SMB_<side>_<n>-<n+1>
                            ALREADY IMPLEMENTED - detector/feb.py:86

  6. FEB power              FEBCrates/Settings/FEBPower[]
                            ALREADY IMPLEMENTED - detector/feb.py:245,
                            including the CrateControllerConnected /
                            CrateControllerEnabled guards

READOUT BRANCH
  R1. Readout board switch  ROSWBF, ROSWBC, ROSWBDS - all three have
                            Settings/readout_scifi and mask_n_scifi
                            STATE: readout_scifi = False on ALL THREE,
                            mask_n_scifi = 0 on all three

  R2. Run parameter         /Experiment/Run Parameters/SciFi readout
                            STATE: False
                            (logged at BOR - see /Logger/Runlog/JSON/
                            "Links BOR"/"SciFi readout")

  R3. Data actually moving  SwitchingFibre sorter/merger counters -
                            see section 4 below

The readout branch was found 2026-07-30 and appears in NO other doc.
It is the "invisible bottleneck" case: the entire power branch can read
green while nothing is being read out.


2) THE INTERLOCK IS READ-ONLY - do not try to write it
--------------------------------------------------------------------
CONFIRMED 2026-07-30 against the dump plus a live screenshot of the
TDKStaging custom page.

The correlation is exact on the Pixel channels:
    Interlock Trigger[i] == True
      <=> Fault Register[i] == 0x0080
      <=> Mode == "OFF" and Output On == False

i.e. the interlock is a PERMISSIVE. When it drops it forces the channel
off and raises the fault. What a client writes is TDKStaging/Settings/
Output On[i] (plus Mode, Demand, Current limit); if the interlock is
down the write does not stick.

There is no ODB command path to raise the interlock. The separate
/Equipment/Interlock tree (new since the 24/07 dump; frontend
/home/mu3e/online/backend_pc/midas_fe/interlock_control/src/
interlock_fe.cxx on mu3ebe, not present in the local online/ checkout)
carries the human-readable REASON - PLC Connected, chillers, helium -
and the per-subdetector permissives Sci/FEC/Pix TDK enable. Those
scalars live under Variables/, which is frontend-owned; treat the whole
tree as display-only.

NOTE on Sci TDK enable: it reads False while the TDKStaging SciFi
channels are on and untriggered. It refers to the FINAL TDK, not the
staging one. Do not wire it as the gate for staging operation.


3) DECISION REVERSAL: DCDCCrates crate-level control
--------------------------------------------------------------------
This section formally reopens custom_page.txt section 3.1's
"DECLINED 2026-07-23" entry, which carried the instruction "noted here
so it isn't proposed again without remembering it was already
explicitly turned down". It is being proposed again, deliberately, for
the reason below.

THE FACTUAL PREMISE WAS WRONG. The declined entry states:

  "a DCDCCrates crate-level on/off (DCDCCrates has no crate-level power
   control at all in the real ODB tree, only per-channel - see 3.2's
   Crate On/Reset/Clear Fault block, which is status+reset, not a power
   switch)"

DCDCCrates/Settings/Crate On is a list of 8, one entry per crate,
indexed by Settings/Device Names = DCDC_C_1 .. DCDC_C_8. It is a
crate-level switch. It was already present, with the same shape, in the
24/07 dump - so this is not something that appeared afterwards.

THE SAFETY PREMISE WAS RIGHT, and is now quantified. Not one of the 8
crates is SciFi-only (from odb_300726_112101.json, 16 channels/crate):

  crate      SciFi channels                     pixel  tile
  DCDC_C_1   SMB_US_2-3, SMB_US_0-1, LDO_US       2     0
  DCDC_C_2   SMB_US_4-5                           3     0
  DCDC_C_3   SMB_US_8-9, SMB_US_6-7               2     0
  DCDC_C_4   SMB_US_10-11                         3     0
  DCDC_C_5   SMB_DS_2-3, SMB_DS_4-5               3     4
  DCDC_C_6   SMB_DS_0-1, LDO_DS                   2     2
  DCDC_C_7   SMB_DS_10-11                         3     4
  DCDC_C_8   SMB_DS_6-7, SMB_DS_8-9               2     4

Turning DCDC_C_7 off from a SciFi page to reclaim one SciFi channel
would drop 3 pixel modules and 2 tile modules.

THE RULE THAT REPLACES THE BLANKET REFUSAL (user's call, 2026-07-30):

  per-channel, where the channel name is unambiguously SciFi's
    => writable with no ceremony
  per-crate, or any other shared unit
    => writable ONLY behind a confirmation that names the non-SciFi
       channels that will be affected

This makes chain levels 2 and 3 (PowerDistStaging, per-channel, names
SMB_*/[US|DS]_LDO/"US DCDC C*") ordinary writable actions, and level 4
(Crate On, per-crate) an informed-consent action. The confirmation text
is generated from the table above, which is why the table lives in this
doc rather than in a chat log.

The authoritative gate stays server-side in Python, per custom_page.txt
section 3.1's existing rule - the page's dialog is UX on top of it, not
the enforcement.


4) THE MONITORING SURFACE
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Swept 2026-07-30 across all 24 top-level ODB trees and all 42 equipment
entries. Everything below reads 0 today because nothing is powered -
these are shapes and paths, not values to trust.

Coverage check performed against custom_page.txt, page_io_reference.txt,
code_design.txt and calibrations_plan.txt. Of the tags below only FCCR
appears in any of them.

4a) PER-FEB TELEMETRY - LinksFibre/Variables/SFFE
    26 fields x 12 FEBs = 312. Names in Settings/"Names SFFE".
      6 temperatures  Arria, MAX, SI1, SI2, ext Arria, DCDC
      5 voltages      1.1, 1.8, 2.5, 3.3, 20
      2 Fireflys x 7  Temperature, Voltage, RX1..RX4 Power, Alarms
    custom_page.txt section 3.1 currently plans four fields for this
    table (crate_voltage_20/3_3/5, feb_temperature). This is the real
    shape. The Firefly RX powers and Alarms are optical-link health -
    they degrade before a link drops.

4b) PER-FEB LINK COUNTERS - LinksFibre/Variables/SFLE (24)
    FEBn LOL Counter, FEBn Error Counter.

4c) PER-CRATE TEMPERATURES - LinksFibre/Variables/SFTS (16)
    Crate N Av FEB Temperature, Crate N Max FEB Temperature, 8 crates.
    Same shape as LinksCentral's SCTS - see the alarm gap in section 5.

4d) PER-FEB DATAPATH - LinksFibre/Variables/"FEB datapath status"
    A SUBTREE, not an array: PLL locked, Buffer full, Frame desync,
    DPA locked, RX ready - 12 entries each.

4e) PER-ASIC READOUT - SciFiFibre/Variables, 16 entries each
    FCCH  hits          FCCF  frame rate
    FCCE  CRC errors    FCCP  PLL Diff
    FCCP is the hook for whatever replaces pll_test.py.

4f) PER-LVDS-LINK - SciFiFibre/Variables/FCLS (408)
    F<n>L<m> Status / Disparity Errors / 8b-10b Errors / Num Hits LVDS.

4g) PER-CHANNEL RATE - SciFiFibre/Variables/FCCR (512)
    "Fibres rate channel N". The only one already known to the docs.

4h) SORTER AND MERGER - SwitchingFibre. THE bottleneck instrumentation.
    Variables/SFSO (408), per FEB and per sorter stage:
      intime hits input 0/1, outoftime hits, overflow hits,
      hits presorter in, hits postsorter in, HITS LOST IN,
      output hits, credits, diag width, delay
    Variables/SFCN (234), event builder / DMA / per FEB:
      FEBn Merger Rate, Reset Phase, Sorter Input/Output,
      LINK FIFO ALMOST FULL / FULL / SORTER LOW / SORTER HIGH,
      EVENT_BUILD_TAG_FIFO_FULL, EVENT_BUILD_SKIP_EVENT_DMA,
      DMA_HALFFUL, GLOBAL_TS_LOW/HIGH
    Variables/"Merger Timeout All FEBs"
    Settings/{Sorter Delay, Sorter Diagnose Offset, Pre Run Configuration}

    "hits lost in" + "LINK FIFO FULL" + "Merger Rate" + "credits"
    together answer AT WHICH STAGE data is being lost. That is the
    single most direct answer to the user's stated goal.

    SwitchingFibre/Commands also carries SciFi-usable commands, mixed in
    with Mupix ones (the frontend is shared with pixel):
      Set FEBs into running / Set FEBs into idle
      Reset Counters
      Set Bypass / Unset Bypass
      Reset SC Main / Reset SC Secondary
      Load Firmware (+ Firmware File, Firmware FEB ID,
                     Firmware Is Emergency Image)
      Run SC Speedtest

4i) COOLING - not mentioned in any page doc before today
    Liquid Cooling SiPM (frontend "Liquid cooling Frontend"):
      Settings/"Names Input" (8): Primary Setpoint, Secondary Setpoint,
        Pump Speed, Internal Temperature, Process Temperature,
        Return Temperature, Fluid pressure, Delivered Power
      Variables/Demand (3) and Variables/"Set State" (2:
        "Temperature control", "Circulation only") are WRITABLE - this
        is operable, not just monitorable
      STATE: all zeros - not connected today
    Chiller manifolds (Status color yellowLight - the only non-green
    equipment in SciFi's set):
      ch 12 SiPMs SciFi supply T (NoConn)   22.59
      ch 13 SiPMs SciFi return T (NoConn)   22.24
      ch 15 SiPMs SciFi return P          1727.08
      ch 17 SiPMs SciFi supply P          3175.18
      The two SciFi temperature sensors are flagged NoConn. The SciTile
      equivalents do read, so the chain works - our sensors are missing.

    WHY THIS MATTERS FOR SCIFI SPECIFICALLY: SiPM breakdown voltage is
    temperature dependent. Running vbr_find/vbr_scan with the SiPM
    chiller off or unstabilised produces a meaningless result and
    leaves HV set for the wrong temperature. Same class of precondition
    as the LV-before-HV pre-check already implemented (code_design.txt
    section 5.4) - but not implemented anywhere.

    STRUCTURAL NOTE: cooling is NOT a rung of the power ladder. It runs
    in parallel and gates HV and calibrations, not LV power-on. Do not
    force it into the linear chain.

4j) ENVIRONMENT - /Equipment/Environment
    64 MSCB sensors plus a computed Dew Point. Almost all named UNCON_*
    and all reading NaN today, so low immediate value - but dew point
    becomes real the day the SiPMs are actually cooled (condensation).
    Worth a placeholder row, not a build.


5) ALARM COVERAGE - and the gap
--------------------------------------------------------------------
/Alarms holds 45 definitions; "Alarm system active" is True. Equipment
actually covered, counted from the conditions:

  FEBCrates 9 · HePlantPiE5 7 · LinksCentral 4 · Environment 3
  Magnet 3 · Liquid Cooling SiPM 2 · Liquid cooling FECs 2
  DCDCCrates 1 · mu3ebe_msysmon 1

  LinksFibre 0 · SciFiFibre 0 · HV 0 · TDK 0 · TDKStaging 0
  PowerDist* 0

SAFETY GAP, found 2026-07-30: THE SCIFI FEBs HAVE NO OVER-TEMPERATURE
ALARM. The "Max FEB Temperature" and "Average FEB Temperature" alarms
read /Equipment/LinksCentral/Variables/SCTS - LinksCentral is the
central switching board. SciFi's FEBs are on LinksFibre, whose SFTS has
the identical shape ("Crate N Av/Max FEB Temperature"). The alarm is
reproducible by copying an existing definition and changing the path.

  existing:  LinksCentral/Variables/SCTS[1,3,5,...] > 65   (Alarm)
             LinksCentral/Variables/SCTS[1,3,5,...] > 60   (Warning)
             LinksCentral/Variables/SCTS[0,2,4,...] > 60   (Alarm, avg)
             LinksCentral/Variables/SCTS[0,2,4,...] > 55   (Warning, avg)
  missing:   the same four against LinksFibre/Variables/SFTS

Also uncovered: HV, TDK and TDKStaging have no alarms at all.

SECOND GAP - a bug in someone else's alarm definitions: "FEB Crate 0
Temperature" and "FEB Crate 1 Temperature" BOTH read
FEBCrates/Variables/SCFC[4]. The rest step by 20 (24, 44, 64, 84, 104,
124, 144). Crate 1 is therefore not monitored - the alarm watches
crate 0's sensor twice. Not ours to fix; report it.

FOUR ALARMS ARE DEFINED BUT INACTIVE, all relevant to us:
  Min SiPM Chiller Temp.  Liquid Cooling SiPM/Variables/Input[3] < 15
  Max SiPM Chiller Temp.  Liquid Cooling SiPM/Variables/Input[3] > 25.00
  Dew Point               Environment/Variables/Dew Point > 8
  TDiode                  DCDCCrates/Variables/Interlock[0-127] > 0.1
Reactivating them is a shared-infrastructure change - ask, do not do.

USABLE VERIFIED CONSTANT: the two SiPM chiller alarms establish a
15-25 C operating window, in the live ODB. That is a real, in-service
number - directly usable where the code currently carries an UNVERIFIED
placeholder. Input[3] is "Internal Temperature".

7) STAGING -> FINAL: parametrise from day one
--------------------------------------------------------------------
The page must target TDKStaging and PowerDistStaging today, and TDK and
PowerDist after installation. The switch is NOT symmetric:

  TDK <-> TDKStaging          Settings/Names are IDENTICAL
                              ("SciFi & Tile US/DS", "FE Crates US/DS",
                              "Pixel US/DS"). Only the equipment name
                              changes.

  PowerDist <-> PowerDistStaging   the CHANNEL NAMES change too:
                              staging  SMB_US_0-1 .. SMB_DS_10-11
                              final    US SciFi5 .. DS SciFi16
                              so the matching PATTERN changes, and with
                              it the channel-to-Feb mapping.

If this is not parametrised from the first version, the page has to be
rebuilt on installation day. Treat the equipment name AND the name
pattern as configuration, not constants.

12) ACTION MODEL - decided while building the phase-3 mockup
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Added 2026-07-30. The mockup (docs/page/chain_mockup.html) carries both
the read-only surface (phase 2) and non-functional action controls
(phase 3) in one file.

12a) NO BUTTON WRITES ANYTHING; EVERY BUTTON PRINTS ITS WRITES.
     Each action panel has a log underneath it. Clicking an action
     renders the exact ODB writes it would perform, e.g.
       /Equipment/PowerDistStaging/Settings/Output On[96] <- 1
     Two reasons this beats a dead button: the interaction can be
     reviewed now, and the log IS the specification the phase-4 Python
     has to implement. Keep this property when the page becomes real -
     a dry-run mode that prints instead of writes is worth having
     permanently, not just as mockup scaffolding.

12b) THE SAFETY RULE IS STRUCTURAL, NOT A WARNING.
     The Tile rows in the Distribution table carry no checkbox at all -
     not a checkbox plus a warning the operator can click past. A
     control that must never be used is a control that should not
     exist. Applies to any future table mixing our channels with
     someone else's in the same equipment tree.

12c) SHARED UNITS GET A CONFIRMATION THAT NAMES THE VICTIMS.
     Crate On and the ROSWB readout_scifi keys open a dialog built from
     section 3's occupancy table: "N pixel and M tile channels will lose
     power - to act on K SciFi channels", with the per-crate breakdown.
     Generated, not written by hand, so it cannot drift from the ODB.
     BOTH directions confirm - switching a shared crate ON energises
     other people's hardware too, which is lower risk but not no risk.

12d) THE PAGE DIALOG IS UX, NOT ENFORCEMENT.
     Stated in the log output itself so it cannot be forgotten: the
     authoritative gate lives in Python and must re-verify the shared-
     channel impact server-side. Consistent with custom_page.txt 3.1's
     existing rule. A page that is stale, bypassed or simply not open
     must not be able to change what is allowed.

12e) PRESET SELECTIONS FOR THE COMMON MOVES.
     "select the 8 crate mains" and "select the 12 SMB pairs" alongside
     select-all. Chain levels 2 and 3 are almost always operated as a
     whole group, and hand-ticking twelve boxes in the right order is
     where a shifter makes a mistake.

12f) WHERE A THRESHOLD IS ALLOWED TO COME FROM.
     If a control ever range-checks a value, the limit must be read
     from something already live in the ODB - the way the 15-25 C SiPM
     window is a pair of real alarm conditions (section 5), not a
     number typed into JS. This is the same rule custom_page.txt 3.1
     states for the UNVERIFIED expected_current/tolerance math, written
     down here in its positive form because the next person adding a
     control will need it.

MOVED, NOT CUT - two tabs folded into custom_page_mockup_v2.html:
  "FEB telemetry" and "Readout health" were both per-FEB views with
  twelve rows keyed by FEB name - i.e. a second copy of a table that
  already exists in the real mockup's Power section. The DATA was not
  the redundancy; the row identity was. Both are now the expanded
  detail of custom_page_mockup_v2.html's existing FEB table, in three
  source-labelled blocks:
    From crate   FEBCrates/Variables/SCFC - the five fields that were
                 already there (crate 20V/3.3V/5V, CC temp, FEB temp)
    From FEB     LinksFibre/Variables/SFFE - 6 temperatures, 5
                 voltages, both Fireflys with RX1-4 powers and alarms
    Datapath     FEB datapath status flags, SFLE LOL/error counters,

  WHY BOTH SOURCES SURVIVE, rather than picking one: they have
  different availability windows. SCFC is read by the crate controller
  over MSCB and works with the FEB dead - which is exactly the
  "why won't this come up" case. SFFE is the FEB's own slow control
  over the optical link and exists only once the FEB is powered and
  linked - which is the running-health case. Neither covers the other's
  moment. An unpowered FEB shows a sentence saying so instead of a wall
  of dashes, so the absence reads as diagnosis rather than missing data.

  NAMING HAZARD, handled: both sources carry a "20 V" and a "3.3 V",
  and both carry a FEB temperature - different rails, different sensors.
  The blocks are labelled with their ODB path and the FEB-side fields
  are prefixed "FEB ..." for exactly this reason. Do not let a future
  edit flatten the three blocks into one list.

  NOT MOVED: the per-ASIC counters (FCCE CRC errors, FCCP PLL diff,
  FCCH hits). They are not per-FEB, so they do not belong in that
  detail row; custom_page_mockup_v2.html is left untouched on this
  point, and they simply no longer appear anywhere. FCCR already exists

14) BRING-UP SEQUENCES - the three procedures
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Dictated by the user 2026-07-30 (HV and LV from their own operating
knowledge; FEB proposed here and NOT yet confirmed). This is what turns
sections 1-3 from a diagnostic ladder into an executable procedure.

STEP KINDS - three, because not every step is "write a path":
  verify-program   a MIDAS program must be running (/Programs/<name>)
  verify-value     something must already be true; no write of ours
  write-verify     write, then read back and confirm it stuck

EVERY STEP CARRIES: name · scope (which indices/channels, DERIVED from
the detector model, never a constant in the page) · precondition ·
action · verification with a timeout · a shared-equipment flag.

--- what falls out of writing them as data --------------------------
  ONE DECLARATION FEEDS THREE CONSUMERS: the page ladder ("where am I
  blocked" = the first step whose verification fails), the bring-up
  button ("run until a step fails, stop there"), and the pre-check
  inside a calibration ("can I start" = does the sequence complete).
  Three implementations today, one source of truth instead.

  POWER-DOWN IS THE SAME LIST REVERSED. The emergency-off path stops
  being three hand-written calls.

  STEPS MUST BE IDEMPOTENT. A step already satisfied is a no-op, so
  "bring up LV" works from any partial state - which is the only state
  a shifter ever actually finds the detector in.

  DRY-RUN IS NOT EXTRA WORK. It is the phase-3 write log
  (section 12a) pointed at a sequence instead of a button.

  WHERE IT LIVES: the engine is subsystem-agnostic and belongs in
  common_scripts - pixel has the same problem, and their /Watchdog
  Conditions are this idea hardcoded. The three declarations are ours
  and belong in scifi_scripts.

  This ABSORBS what earlier notes called the "chain class": same
  object, executable rather than merely diagnostic.

17) THE WEATHER STATIONS - what they are and what they gate
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Added 2026-07-30 after the four "WEATHER" channels turned up in
PowerDistStaging's "Slow Control" device and nobody could say what they
were for.

STRUCTURE, from the dump. /Equipment/Environment is four stations of 16
channels each - US_TOP1, US_TOP2, DS_TOP1, US_BOTTOM1. Every station is
8 direct temperature inputs (T0..T7) plus two expansion ports, each
port carrying T0, T1, RH and O2. So a station measures TEMPERATURE,
RELATIVE HUMIDITY and OXYGEN.

INFERENCE, not proven: the four PowerDistStaging channels named
US/DS WEATHER TOP/BOTTOM (ch 100, 101, 116, 117 - on, 0.02-0.04 A) are
what powers these boards. It rests on the count matching and on the
US/DS + top/bottom naming axis. Note it is NOT clean: Environment has
no DS_BOTTOM station, while a "DS WEATHER BOTTOM" power channel exists.

WHAT THEY ARE FOR, in the order that matters to SciFi:

  1. DECIDING WHETHER IT IS SAFE TO COOL. If a cooled surface drops
     below the dew point of the air around it, water condenses on it.
     Condensation on an HV-biased SiPM board, or on FEB electronics,
     means leakage current and damage. The SiPM chiller window is
     15-25 C (section 5) - but whether that window is SAFE is decided
     by the humidity in the cage, which only these boards know.
     So the real precondition chain is
        weather station -> RH / dew point in limits -> chiller -> HV, VBR
     The weather station does not gate HV. It gates the COOLING, and
     the cooling gates HV and VBR. One rung further up the parallel
     branch of section 4i.
     Wired in the ODB as: alarms "Env RH Top US" and "Env RH Top DS"
     (> 70%), plus a computed Dew Point with an alarm above 8 C.

  2. AN INDEPENDENT MEASUREMENT ON THE COOLING LOOPS. The 8 channels
     that carry a real name are not ambient air, they are attached to
     things: US_TOP1_T1_HE_INLET_VTX, US_TOP1_T4_HE_INLET_OUTER,
     US_TOP1_T5_TL_US_OUT, US_TOP2_T4_TL_US_IN, DS_TOP1_T1_DCDC_C_6,
     DS_TOP1_T4_DS_TL_OUT, US_BOTTOM1_T0_US_SC_OUT,
     US_BOTTOM1_T1_US_SC_IN. The chiller reports its own internal
     temperature; these report the temperature of what the detector
     actually sits on. If the two disagree, something is wrong between
     the chiller and the load.

  3. PERSONNEL SAFETY - OXYGEN. Mu3e uses helium and helium displaces
     air. Every station port has an O2 channel, 8 in total. NO ALARM
     READS ANY OF THEM. The only O2 alarm in the system is
     "Helium piE5 O2" on HePlantPiE5/Variables/MSCB[16], and it is
     inactive.

DO THEY MATTER FOR LV AND HV? For turning either ON, no - no step of
either sequence (section 14) reads Environment, and LV has its own
seven per-channel hardware flags plus TDiode. The distinction is
between POWERING and OPERATING:
  LV  effectively never. The single link is DS_TOP1_T1_DCDC_C_6, a
      temperature on one DCDC crate - redundancy on the crate, not a
      precondition.
  HV  not to switch on, but to run safely: HV lives on cooled SiPMs,
      and cooling is what creates the condensation risk these boards
      guard. VBR's own dependence is on TEMPERATURE (the chiller), not
      on humidity - the weather station's job there is only to say the
      chiller may be run.

STATE TODAY: all 64 channels read NaN and 56 of 64 are named UNCON_ /
UCON_, i.e. not connected. The two RH alarms are formally active and
are watching a NaN. The read-out hardware is powered - the WEATHER
channels draw current - but the sensors do not arrive. Nothing here is
protecting anything yet.

TWO CONFIGURATION ERRORS, to report to whoever owns Environment:
  a) Settings/"T for DewP" and Settings/"RH for DewP" are BOTH set to
     US_TOP1_T1_HE_INLET_VTX. A dew point needs a temperature and a
     humidity; the humidity source points at a temperature channel. The
     computed Dew Point is NaN regardless.
  b) "Env RH Top US" reads MSCB[46] = UNCON_DS_TOP1_Port3_RH and
     "Env RH Top DS" reads MSCB[42] = UNCON_DS_TOP1_Port2_RH. Both are
     DS_TOP1 - the "US" alarm watches a DS sensor, and the two watch
     two ports of the same station. Everything involved is unconnected
     and NaN, so this is probably provisional rather than a live bug,
     but as written it is wrong.

WHEN THE SENSORS ARRIVE, the checklist is: sensors connected and
reading -> the two RH alarms pointed at the right stations -> dew point
computed from a real T and a real RH -> the Dew Point alarm activated
-> only then run the SiPM chiller cold. An alarm on NaN never fires, so
none of the current alarm coverage means anything until step one.
